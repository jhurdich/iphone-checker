StockScout Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

StockScout helps you save iPhone searches for later. This policy explains what information the service uses and how you can request its deletion.

Information we store

If you sign in with Google, StockScout receives your Google account identifier, email address, and name when Google provides them. We use this information to authenticate your account and associate saved searches with it. StockScout does not request access to Gmail, Drive, contacts, or other Google services.

When you save a search, StockScout stores the iPhone model, color, storage size, and location you entered, along with a creation time. A secure session cookie keeps you signed in. The cookie expires after 30 days.

How information is used

We use this information to provide sign-in, display your saved searches, and let you remove searches. StockScout does not sell this information or use it for advertising. Search details are not sent to Apple by StockScout; the Apple link opens Apple's website so you can check pickup availability there.

Service providers and storage

Google provides sign-in. Cloudflare hosts the site and stores account and search information in its database service. These providers process information to operate the service.

Retention and deletion

Saved searches remain in your account until you remove them. To request deletion of your account information, contact jhurdich@gmail.com from the email address associated with your account. Session cookies expire after 30 days or when you sign out.

Children and changes

StockScout is not designed for children under 13. We may update this policy as the service changes. The latest version will be posted on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, email jhurdich@gmail.com.

StockScout is an independent project and is not affiliated with Apple.
